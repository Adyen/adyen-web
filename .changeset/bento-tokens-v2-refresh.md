Improved: Upgraded to Bento design tokens v2, refreshing the default colours, corner radii and font weights, and moving the toggle, alert and icon-only button styles to core tokens. The pay button now uses `--adyen-sdk-color-background-inverse-primary`, `--adyen-sdk-color-background-inverse-primary-hover` and `--adyen-sdk-color-background-inverse-primary-active`; the previous `--adyen-sdk-color-background-always-dark` and `--adyen-sdk-color-background-always-dark-active` overrides are deprecated but still applied to the pay button as fallbacks.
